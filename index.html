Questions we hear a lot

How fast can you arrive in an emergency?

Usually within 60 minutes in our main service areas, any time of day or night.

Do you charge for inspection?

The visit is free when you go ahead with the repair. Otherwise a small inspection fee applies, which we tell you before we come.

Do you give a warranty?

Yes. 30 days on labor and the manufacturer's warranty on parts we supply.

Do you bring your own materials?

Yes. We carry common pipes and fittings. Material cost is shown separately on your quote.

What should I do before the plumber arrives?

Close the main water valve, switch off the geyser and electric pump, and move belongings away from the water.

Do you work on weekends and holidays?

Yes, every day including public holidays.
